02 / About

Music, visuals,
and technology.

I DJ, create visuals, and get into the technical side of live shows.

I started DJing in VR during the 2020 pandemic. I now help run a virtual rave community as a leader and staff member, and regularly host live events there.

Alongside mixing, I do graphics engineering, rig DMX lighting, and experiment with unconventional controllers. I like figuring out how to make the visuals and lighting work with the music.

I mainly play house and garage, but I’ve also spent a lot of time mixing music for general audiences. I’m available for EDM gigs, weddings, and other events, as well as VR performances.
